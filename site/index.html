The desktop

OpenStation makes WordPress feel like a computer you own.

Movable windows, a dock, and a workspace that feels like yours — without replacing the WordPress underneath it.

The app shelf

ODD fills that desktop with things worth opening.

Browse the Shop, install an app, and launch it in a native OpenStation window. No admin-page detour required.

Your data

WordPress stays in charge of the important stuff.

ODD Notes saves private notes in your site, with recovery and revision history. Your desktop is not another SaaS account.

First on the shelf

ODD Notes gives good thoughts a quiet place to land.

Write without leaving your desktop. Search, tag, favorite, archive, pin, and share when you choose. Your notes stay in WordPress, with local recovery and revision history for the moments when things go sideways.
